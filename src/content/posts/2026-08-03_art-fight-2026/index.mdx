# 2026 Art Fight Season

![Screenshot of the art fight website, showing the 3 teams and their colored icons--all stylized theatre masks](teams.png "Hats off to the Art Fight admin team because the team art is always killer every year")

This year the event was a little different! For the first time it was a 3-team arrangement. Teams **Comedy**, **Tragedy**, and **Mystery**.\
As usually I don't really care that much about the actual competition, it's just fun set-dressing.

I picked Tragedy! I don't think we're gonna win!! So many people picked the *clown* team (comedy) because of course.

## Life Context

This was a tough year for me. My girlfriend and I have been battling the Chicago dog groomer job market for months now, and this was the month that our last ditch effort to land her a stable, dignified job fell through.

Suffice to say we were incredibly stressed until the very end of the month. That, plus Anthrocon in Pittsburgh taking up the weekend of the 4th of July...I had a lot of things taking my time.

That being said I still put out 12 pieces that I'm incredibly proud of.

### 5. Jelly

![A fuzzy green anthro worm on a string character, bashfully sitting in the palm of a yellow and pink hand, holding its tail. The background is a vague foresty background with some shelf fungus and leaves floating in the background](@assets/myArt/artFight2026/Jelly.png)

I knew I wanted to attack my very good friend Briva, but picking a character from her incredible roster is so hard...Ultimately I settled on Jelly because I'm obsessed with how she goofily breaks the rules. Is she average height? Or tiny? Hard to tell!

I found a cute picture of a shrew resting in someone's palm and felt inspired, so that became the reference for this. Of course I made Briva's fursona the hand holding her too.

The pose and anatomy tripped me up a few times but I'm very happy with the cute result!

Proud of:
1. The idea and execution
2. Cuteness
3. Selling the scene

Room for improvement: 
1. Fluff/fur texture
2. Expressions
3. Shading

### 6. Levi

![An anthro blue and orange velociraptor in swordsman clothes and leather armor. He has a tired expression, and is sighing as he holds a cup of hot tea. He sits at a bar or table in an implied tavern or bar.](@assets/myArt/artFight2026/Levi.png)

While I'm still proud of this piece, it was easily my biggest struggle this season. I think my approach of translating my friend's comic style into my own was a bit ham-fisted and I should've loosened up in my approach.

I think I did a good job respecting the character's lore, role, etc. though! I tried to depict him in a between-adventures setting and I like how it came together, even as I was trying to keep myself from over-obsessing the piece.

Proud of:

1. Scene/setting
2. Gritty shading

Room for improvement:

1. Facial expression
2. Posing
3. Stylization and scalie features

### 10. Nicanor

![Extremely muscular dark grey komodo dragon martial artist in a fighting pose. He has a tattered yellow headband tied around his head and is wearing black shorts with some colored stripes. The background is a simple geometric shape with striped colors](https://artfight.net/attack/18208838.nicanor)

In terms of effort, output, and finished product, I think this is my "big" piece for this year. I just really wanted to go all out on a BUFF character and really pull out all the stops, and I think I really pulled it off here.

That being said, I still restrained myself a bit--limiting time overall and rejecting some of my initial, more ambitious pose ideas.

I'm very proud of myself for pushing the muscle anatomy a bit past my comfort zone for a piece like this, and I love love how the shading turned out--especially for the veins and shoulder shine. It's a technique I'll definitely be bringing with me past Art Fight.

I also think I did a good job with the facial expression! Komodo dragon character (mine included) have pretty simple faces, so I'm happy with how much personality I was able to impart with the few details I was working with.

### 11. Keel

![Small, yellow kobold excitedly swinging off of a large pink naga character's rippling biceps](@assets/myArt/artFight2026/Keel.png)

Another annual tradition: attacking Somite! Lol. Somite has some really fun characters that I just really enjoy challenging myself with. The hardest part each year is choosing who to draw!

This time I challenged myself by drawing Keel, a kobold. Why is that challenging? I feel I have a weakness in drawing small characters and imparting _chaotic_ energy.\
The pose I picked really helped with both of those!

Even though it was a tough pose, I vibed it out pretty well. There really weren't great references for this, so I'm quite pleased. Plus it served as an excuse to draw another BUFF SCALIE helllll yeahhh

Also shout out to [Rayzr](https://bsky.app/profile/did:plc:vcvmi6adbzn6rtksqhjogq73) whose advice on how to draw decent scalie feet keeps proving useful. It wasn't the focus of this piece, but I think it really helped shore up a BIG weakness of mine.

### 12. Juniper

![Headshot of a pretty ram girl with deep tan skin and fluffy yellow wool](@assets/myArt/artFight2026/Juniper.png)

A well-overdue attack on my good friend Juni! I really genuinely struggled to get the "ram" shape right (to my eye, at least). I'm happy with where it ended up but my word this was very out of my wheelhouse. Definitely need more practice!

I don't have an issue with headshots, but as an anatomy guy I love drawing bodies. This is a headshot purely because of time constraints, as my final Art Fight.

I think I did a good job though, aside from how dark it turned out (my iPad playing color tricks on me again).\
Excited to do even better with next year's attack >:3

Proud of:

1. Expression
2. "She looks like a ram"

Room for improvement:

1. Color
2. Non-scalie expressive faces

## Conclusion

Once again life got busy and complicated, and Art Fight was a welcome, solitary blast of art and creativity. I don't feel I jumped much in skill between now and last year, but that's okay. I'm still having fun, and still trying new things.

9 years!! Crazy. I love this event. It's so simple and fun, but I really look forward to it each year.
